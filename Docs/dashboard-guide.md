# Dashboard Guide

## Page 1 — Executive Overview

Purpose: show the overall business situation quickly.

Focus on revenue, orders, customers, average order value, growth and major trends.

## Page 2 — Sales Analysis

Purpose: understand where revenue and orders are coming from.

Typical analysis:
- Monthly revenue
- Revenue by category
- Orders by category
- Revenue by state
- Average Order Value
- MoM growth

## Page 3 — Customers & RFM

Purpose: understand customer value and retention opportunities.

RFM means:
- **Recency** — how recently a customer purchased
- **Frequency** — how often they purchased
- **Monetary** — how much they spent

Focus on repeat customers, RFM segments, segment revenue and at-risk customers.

## Page 4 — Delivery & Logistics

Purpose: understand fulfillment performance.

Main KPIs:
- On-Time Delivery %
- Late Delivery %
- Average Delivery Days
- Average Delivery Delay Days
- Average Freight per Order

Main visuals:
- Monthly delivery performance
- Delivery status
- Average delivery time by month
- Freight cost vs delivery delay
- Top states by late deliveries

## Page 5 — Key Business Insights

This page should not repeat every chart.

Use short sections:
- Executive Summary
- Sales Insights
- Customer & RFM Insights
- Delivery Insights
- Geographic Insight
- Business Recommendations

The purpose is to answer:

> What did the analysis tell us, and what should the business do next?

## Design Principles

- Keep the same visual language across pages.
- Keep KPI cards aligned.
- Use whitespace instead of unnecessary visuals.
- Use bar charts for category comparisons.
- Use line charts for trends.
- Keep titles business-oriented.
- Keep the dashboard focused rather than adding pages just for the sake of page count.
