# Key Business Insights

## Executive Summary

- **15.49M** delivered revenue from **96.48K orders**
- **160.55** average order value
- **4.33%** MoM revenue growth
- Only **3.12%** of customers are repeat customers
- **92.07%** of orders were delivered on time
- Customer retention and delivery optimization are the main opportunities

## Sales Insights

### Health & Beauty Leads Revenue
Health & Beauty generated approximately **1.42M**, followed by Watches & Gifts at **1.27M** and Bed Bath & Table at **1.24M**.

### Revenue Shows Significant Monthly Variation
Monthly revenue reached around **1.7M in May**, dropped to roughly **0.7M in September**, and recovered later in the year.

## Customer & RFM Insights

### Repeat Customer Rate Is Very Low
Only **2.91K of 93.36K customers** are repeat customers, resulting in a repeat customer rate of about **3.12%**.

### At-Risk Customers Represent Significant Revenue
At-Risk customers account for approximately **23.45%** of customers and contribute around **5.3M** in revenue.

## Delivery Insights

### Delivery Reliability Is Strong
**92.07%** of orders were delivered on time, while **7.93%** were late. Average delivery time was **12.01 days**.

### Delivery Speed Improved Then Deteriorated
Average delivery time fell to around **8.6 days in August**, but increased to about **14.8 days by December**.

## Geographic Insight

### São Paulo Is the Largest Market and Biggest Delivery Risk
São Paulo generated approximately **5.8M** in revenue and recorded **2,387** late deliveries, making it a priority for logistics optimization.

## Business Recommendations

1. Improve customer retention with targeted re-engagement campaigns.
2. Protect At-Risk revenue by prioritizing high-value customers.
3. Investigate São Paulo logistics bottlenecks and carrier performance.
4. Prepare additional delivery capacity for year-end pressure.

> These are analytical findings, not causal claims. A correlation or concentration in the dashboard does not by itself prove the root cause.
